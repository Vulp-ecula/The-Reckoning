☠ the order of play ▸

  1. Draw. Each turn brings a free Peasant and a card from your own deck.
  2. Play. Click a card, then click an empty lane. (Dragging works too.)
  3. Pay. 🩸 means sacrifice that many of your own creatures — click them on the table. Marrow will not buy blood.
  4. Relics. Click one on the left. Once per fight, then it returns at the next seat.
  5. Ring the bell. Each creature strikes the lane facing it. Hit an empty lane and the souls are yours.
  6. Win. First to 35 souls takes the round; best of three takes the seat. Beat all three seats to face the last one.
